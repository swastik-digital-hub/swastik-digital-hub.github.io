🌱 Chapter 1: The Living World Overview

Fundamental concepts of biodiversity, characteristics of living organisms, and taxonomic hierarchy guidelines for Class 11 and NEET prep:

  • Defining Features of Life: Metabolism, cellular organization, and consciousness are defining features, whereas growth and reproduction have exceptions.
  • Binomial Nomenclature: System developed by Carl Linnaeus. Generic name begins with a capital letter, species epithet with a small letter (italicized or underlined).
  • Taxonomic Hierarchy: Kingdom → Phylum/Division → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species.
  • Taxonomical Aids: Herbarium, Botanical Gardens, Museums, Zoological Parks, and Keys (couplet & lead analysis).
