🦁 High-Yield NEET Zoology Core Revision Summary

Targeted concepts, physiological pathways, and evolutionary insights mandatory for scoring 180/180 in NEET Zoology:

  • Human Reproduction & Endocrine Control: GnRH triggers LH and FSH release from the anterior pituitary. LH stimulates Leydig cells to produce testosterone, while FSH acts on Sertoli cells for spermiogenesis.
  • Neural Control & Coordination: Resting membrane potential of neurons is maintained at -70mV by Na+/K+ ATPase pumps (3 Na+ out for 2 K+ in). Depolarization occurs due to rapid influx of Na+ ions.
  • Immunity & Immunoglobulins: IgA is present in colostrum, IgG crosses the placenta for passive immunity, and IgE mediates allergic responses via histamine release from mast cells.
  • Biotechnology & Recombinant DNA: Restriction enzymes (molecular scissors) cut at palindromic sequences; DNA ligase seals phosphodiester bonds, while PCR amplifies genes using Taq Polymerase.
