🌱 High-Yield NEET Botany Core Revision Summary

Targeted metabolic cycles, anatomical structures, and genetic principles mandatory for securing top scores in NEET Botany:

  • Photosynthesis & C4 Pathway: C4 plants (like maize and sugarcane) utilize PEP carboxylase in mesophyll cells for initial $CO_2$ fixation, avoiding photorespiration by concentrating $CO_2$ in bundle sheath cells via the Hatch-Slack pathway.
  • Plant Growth Regulators (Phytohormones): Auxin promotes apical dominance, Gibberellins stimulate bolting in rosette plants, Cytokinins delay leaf senescence (Richmond-Lang effect), and Abscisic Acid (ABA) acts as a stress hormone closing stomata.
  • Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants: Microsporogenesis produces pollen grains containing a vegetative cell and a generative cell. Double fertilization involves syngamy (forming a diploid zygote) and triple fusion (forming a triploid primary endosperm nucleus).
  • Molecular Genetics & DNA Fingerprinting: Transcription in eukaryotes involves RNA Polymerase II synthesizing mRNA, followed by post-transcriptional modifications including capping, tailing, and splicing.
Botany Conceptual Modules & Syllabus Highlights

Plant Physiology & Transport

Detailed analysis of water potential equations, transpiration pull theory, active/passive mineral absorption, and pressure flow hypothesis in phloem translocation.

Photosynthesis & Cellular Respiration

Comprehensive review of cyclic and non-cyclic photophosphorylation, Calvin cycle energetics, glycolysis, Krebs cycle, and the electron transport chain (ETC).

Plant Anatomy & Morphology

In-depth study of meristematic tissues, secondary growth in dicot stems (vascular cambium and cork cambium), root/stem modifications, and inflorescence types.

Genetics & Plant Breeding

Exploration of Mendelian inheritance deviations, chromosomal theory of inheritance, linkage mapping, polygenic inheritance, and crop improvement strategies.
